F · Rules (binding for every design above)

One master image per item — 1200×630, family gradient (quiz purple · riddle teal · joke amber · game accent), content inside the central SAFE ZONE. Answer visibility: quiz/riddle questions share without answers; dad jokes share with the punchline; image riddles share the photo only. Options in image follow the question type (2/3/4/open) and mirror the site's 2-column grid; correct option never marked. Text: title ≤ 60 · description ≤ 110 · WhatsApp message carries the challenge + options + link. Numbers are live: subject/category counts and home totals come from the DB and update automatically; the count sits in the image URL so platforms refetch. URLs are durable (subject/category/game), never rotting session links. Games stay dependency-free; bump ?v=N when editing their files.

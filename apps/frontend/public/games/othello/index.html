Othello ⚫⚪

Outflank to flip. Whoever owns more discs when the board fills wins.

Mode

Difficulty

Board

Place a disc that outflanks a straight line of your opponent's discs — they all flip to your colour at once. You must flip with every move; if you cannot, you pass. When neither side can move, the player with more discs wins.

Quad-Nim — avoid the last stick

22 sticks, take one to three each turn. Whoever is forced to eat the last stick finishes 4th — and the player who pushed them into it takes the win. Do the maths: leave one more than a multiple of four.

Sticks left: aim to leave 1, 5, 9, 13, 17 or 21 after your move. The last stick is poison.

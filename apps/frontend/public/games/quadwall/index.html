Quadwall — race home, wall the rest

Multiplayer Quoridor: every pawn races to the opposite edge. Drop two-cell walls to slow the others — but a wall may never make anyone's goal unreachable. Three players get 6 walls each, four get 5. First pawn home wins.

Tap a highlighted square to move. Pick a wall mode, then tap a slot to drop it. Jumps hop over neighbours.

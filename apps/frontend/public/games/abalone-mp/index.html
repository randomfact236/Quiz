Abalone-3 — sumo on the hex ring

Three marble triangles on the sixty-one-space ring. Move one to three of your marbles in a line: push enemy groups strictly smaller than yours (sideways slides need clear lanes), and marbles shoved off the edge are ejected. First to eject four marbles wins.

Tap your marbles (up to three in a line), then a direction. The arrows point along the six hex axes.

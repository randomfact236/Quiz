Komi

A stone's liberties are the empty points touching its group. Fill a group's last liberty and it comes off the board. You may not fill your own last liberty (a suicide) unless the move captures. The simple ko rule bars a move that recreates the position just before yours. Both players pass and the game is scored: your stones plus the empty points only you touch, plus komi to white.

Pentago — place a marble, twist a quadrant

Tap an empty square, then twist any quadrant a quarter turn. First player to line up five of their colour after their turn wins — the board spins under everyone's feet.

Tap a marble spot first, then pick a twist. Quadrants are marked by the thicker lines (Q1 top-left, Q2 top-right, Q3 bottom-left, Q4 bottom-right).

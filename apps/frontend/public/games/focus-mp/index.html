Focus — stack, merge, capture by height

Enter pieces from your hand onto any open square, then move the top piece or a whole stack one square. Land on an enemy stack with an equal-or-taller group and it is captured for good. Lose every piece and you are out — last player with pieces wins.

Tap your stack to pick it up, then a lit square. Tap an empty square to enter a new piece from your hand.
